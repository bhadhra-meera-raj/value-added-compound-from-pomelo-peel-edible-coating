# Extraction of Value-Added Compound from Pomelo Peel and Its Application as an Edible Coating

## Overview
This project focused on the extraction, characterization and optimisation of pectin from pomelo peel and its application as an edible coating.

## Objectives
- Extract pectin from pomelo peel
- Characterize the extracted pectin
- Optimize the extraction process using Design of Experiments (DOE)
- Apply the extracted pectin as an edible coating
- Shelf life study of coated and uncoated fruit

## Methodology
## Methodology
Pectin was extracted from pomelo peel under controlled experimental conditions. The effects of selected extraction parameters were evaluated and optimized using Design of Experiments (DOE) with a Box-Behnken Design. The experimental results were analyzed using ANOVA to assess the significance of process variables and their interactions. The extracted pectin was subsequently characterized and evaluated for its application as an edible coating.
